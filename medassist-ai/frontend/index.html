How it works

  1. 1 Extract text with PyMuPDF.
  2. 2 Split content into overlapping chunks.
  3. 3 Create local sentence-transformer embeddings.
  4. 4 Store vectors in persistent ChromaDB.
  5. 5 Retrieve relevant passages for each question.
